Distant Garden Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy covers the Distant Garden game for iPhone and iPad, including TestFlight builds.

Short version

Distant Garden has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game does not send your personal data to the developer.

Data stored on your device

Your worlds, settings and saved game data are stored on your own device. The developer does not receive them.

Multiplayer

Multiplayer is optional. When you host or join a world, your player name, position and in-game actions are shared with the other players in that world. Hosted worlds run on a player's device, not on servers operated by the developer.

Text chat

If you use text chat, your messages are sent to the other players in the same world, through the host. The game does not save chat history. Players can block and report other players in the game.

Voice chat and microphone

Voice chat is optional. The game asks for microphone access the first time you use the Talk button, and uses the microphone only while you hold it. Your voice is sent live to nearby players in the same world, through the host. The game does not record or save voice audio.

Local network

The game may use your local network to host and join multiplayer games with nearby devices.

Apple

If you install the game through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple collects its own information, such as installation data, crash reports and tester feedback, under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer may see crash reports and feedback you choose to send through TestFlight.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through TestFlight feedback or the developer's page on the store where you got the game.
